**Date:** 2026-09-09 · **Status:** nothing written or published. Queue only.

**Governing rule:** every item below states the exact evidence required *before writing begins*. Missing evidence blocks the item — it is never grounds to generalise, infer or invent. A blocked item stays blocked.

**Standing constraints for all items:**
- Samrat FRP Traders is a **trader / supplier / importer / reseller**. Never a manufacturer.
- No "ISO certified", "authorised distributor", "factory direct", "pan-Nepal stock", "best", or guaranteed delivery/stock claims.
- No chemical, performance, curing, storage, safety or compatibility claim unless it comes from supplier TDS/SDS **with attribution**, or written approval from a qualified technical representative.
- No prices, no stock levels, no fabricated reviews, testimonials, photography or certifications.
- Every page ships in **both** Nepali and English.
